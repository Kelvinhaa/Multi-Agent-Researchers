# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how Harbourline Freight Systems engineering classifies,
acknowledges and resolves production incidents, and the internal targets
each severity is held to.

## Severity levels and targets

Incidents are classified P1, P2 or P3 on declaration. P1 incidents must be
acknowledged internally within 15 minutes and are targeted for resolution
within 4 hours. P2 incidents must be acknowledged within 1 hour and are
targeted for resolution within 12 hours. P3 incidents must be acknowledged
within 4 hours and are targeted for resolution within 5 business days.
These are internal engineering targets, distinct from any customer-facing
commitment set out in a service agreement.

| Severity | Acknowledgement | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | 1 hour | 12 hours |
| P3 | 4 hours | 5 business days |

## Incident commander

Every P1 incident requires a named incident commander, assigned as soon as
the incident is declared, who coordinates the response and is the single
point of contact for status updates until the incident is resolved. P2 and
P3 incidents do not require a dedicated incident commander, though the
on-call engineer remains responsible for driving them to resolution.

## Status page

For P1 incidents, an update must be posted to the public status page within
30 minutes of declaration, and at regular intervals thereafter until
resolution. Status page updates for P2 and P3 incidents are at the incident
commander's or on-call engineer's discretion.

## Post-incident review

Every P1 incident receives a written post-incident review covering root
cause, impact and follow-up actions, coordinated by the incident commander.
P2 incidents receive a review where the incident commander judges it
valuable; P3 incidents typically do not.
