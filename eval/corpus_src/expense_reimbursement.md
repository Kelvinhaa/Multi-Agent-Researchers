# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how Harbourline Freight Systems staff claim and are
reimbursed for legitimate business expenses incurred outside the corporate
card system, and what supporting documentation each claim requires.

## Submitting a claim

Expense claims must be submitted through the Finance Portal within 30 days of
the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after this window are
returned to the employee and require their manager's written justification
before Finance will process them, so staff should lodge claims promptly
rather than batching several months of spend into a single submission.

| Step | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Submission window | Within 30 days of spend |
| Approval | Employee's manager |
| Payment | Next scheduled pay run |

## Receipts and documentation

A receipt or tax invoice must be attached to any claim line above $75. Claims
at or below $75 may be submitted with a brief description of the expense and
its business purpose, but Finance may still request a receipt for audit
purposes. Claims lacking required receipts are held pending documentation
rather than rejected outright.

## Reimbursement timing

Approved claims are paid in the ordinary pay run on the 15th of each month.
Claims approved after the cut-off for that pay run roll over automatically
to the following month's run. Employees do not need to resubmit a claim that
has already been approved but missed a cut-off.

## Ineligible expenses

Personal expenses, fines and penalties, and any spend that has already been
covered by a corporate card or a travel advance are not eligible for
reimbursement under this policy. Employees who are uncertain whether an
expense qualifies should check with their manager before incurring the cost.
