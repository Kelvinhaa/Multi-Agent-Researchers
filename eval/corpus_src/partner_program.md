# Partner Program Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes the tiers of Harbourline Freight Systems' partner
program, the referred revenue required to reach each tier, the margin a
partner earns, and how long a registered deal remains protected.

## Partner tiers

The partner program has three tiers: Registered, Silver and Gold. Partners
enter the program at Registered tier and progress to Silver and then Gold
as their referred annual recurring revenue grows. Reaching Gold tier
requires a partner to have referred $500,000 in annual recurring revenue,
assessed on a rolling basis.

| Tier | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Registered | Entry tier |
| Silver | Referred ARR milestone below Gold |
| Gold | $500,000 referred ARR |

## Margins

Each tier earns a different margin on deals the partner originates.
Registered partners earn a 15% margin, Silver partners earn a 22% margin,
and Gold partners earn a 30% margin. Margin is paid once the referred
contract is signed and invoiced.

| Tier | Margin |
|---|---|
| Registered | 15% |
| Silver | 22% |
| Gold | 30% |

## Deal registration

A partner registers a deal with Harbourline before engaging a prospect, to
protect their claim to the margin on that opportunity. A registered deal
remains valid for 90 days from registration. If the deal has not closed
within that window, the partner must re-register it to retain protection,
which also gives the partner program team a chance to confirm the
opportunity is still active.

## Tier review

Partner tier is reviewed periodically against the referred ARR thresholds
above, and a partner's tier may move up or down to reflect their current
referred revenue.
