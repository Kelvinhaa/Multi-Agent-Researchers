# Business Travel Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out meal, accommodation and flight class entitlements for
staff travelling on Harbourline Freight Systems business, whether domestic
or international.

## Meal allowances

Travelling staff receive a daily meal allowance of $85 for domestic travel
and $120 for international travel, intended to cover breakfast, lunch and
dinner while away from their home office. The allowance is paid regardless
of actual spend and does not require individual meal receipts, though the
overall trip must still be supported by an approved travel itinerary.

| Travel type | Daily meal allowance |
|---|---|
| Domestic | $85 |
| International | $120 |

## Accommodation

Hotel spend is capped by location to reflect local market rates. In Sydney
and Melbourne, the nightly cap is $280. Everywhere else within Australia and
New Zealand, the nightly cap is $220. Staff booking above these caps need
prior approval from their manager, for example where no suitable room is
available below the cap for a particular date.

| Location | Nightly cap |
|---|---|
| Sydney or Melbourne | $280 |
| Elsewhere domestic | $220 |

## Flights

Economy class is the standard for all business travel. Business class is
permitted only for flights with a scheduled duration over 4 hours, and only
for members of the Executive Leadership Team. All other staff travel economy
regardless of flight length, though exit-row or extra-legroom seating may be
booked at the traveller's discretion within the standard fare.

## Booking and approval

All travel must be booked through the Finance Portal's travel booking tool
and approved by the traveller's manager before tickets are purchased.
Personal travel combined with a business trip is permitted, but any
additional cost, including changed flight dates, is borne by the employee.
