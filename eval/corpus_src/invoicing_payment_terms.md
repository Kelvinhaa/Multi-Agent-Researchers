# Invoicing and Payment Terms Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets Harbourline Freight Systems' standard invoicing terms for
customers, the late payment fee applied to overdue invoices, and when a
purchase order is required before an invoice can be raised.

## Standard payment terms

Invoices to standard customers are payable within 30 days of the invoice
date. Customers on an enterprise agreement receive extended terms of 45
days, reflecting the longer internal approval cycles typical of larger
organisations. Payment terms are set out on the face of every invoice and
in the customer's master services agreement.

| Customer tier | Payment terms |
|---|---|
| Standard | 30 days |
| Enterprise | 45 days |

## Late payment

An invoice not paid within its payment terms attracts a late fee of 1.5% of
the outstanding balance per month, calculated from the invoice due date
until payment is received in full. The late fee is disclosed in the master
services agreement and applied automatically by the billing system; it is
not applied at Finance's discretion.

## Purchase orders

A purchase order is required before Harbourline will raise an invoice for
any order above $2,500. Orders at or below $2,500 may be invoiced against a
signed quote alone. Customers who cannot supply a purchase order above this
threshold should contact their account manager to agree an alternative
before work begins.

## Disputed invoices

A customer disputing part of an invoice must raise the dispute with their
account manager before the payment due date. Disputing part of an invoice
does not extend the payment terms for the undisputed portion, which remains
payable on the original due date.
