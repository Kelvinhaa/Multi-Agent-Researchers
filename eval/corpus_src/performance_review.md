# Performance Review Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how Harbourline Freight Systems runs performance
reviews, how ratings are calibrated, and how promotions are nominated.

## Review cycle

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice yearly, in March and
September. Each cycle includes a self-assessment, manager assessment and a
short calibration discussion. Informal check-ins between an employee and
their manager are encouraged throughout the year but do not replace the two
formal cycles.

| Cycle | Month |
|---|---|
| First half | March |
| Second half | September |

## Rating scale

Reviews use a 1–5 rating scale, where 3 means "meets expectations." A rating
of 1 or 2 triggers a support plan agreed between the employee and their
manager, while a rating of 4 or 5 is required for an employee to be
considered for promotion in that cycle.

## Calibration

Ratings are calibrated before release to employees. Each department's ratings
are reviewed by a calibration panel of 3 department heads, who compare
ratings across teams to ensure consistent standards before a manager
finalises and shares the result with their report.

## Promotion nominations

Managers nominate employees for promotion alongside each review cycle.
Nominations close on 14 February for the March cycle and on 14 August for the
September cycle. Late nominations are held over to the following cycle.
