# Remote and Flexible Work Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets expectations for hybrid and remote work at Harbourline
Freight Systems, including core collaboration hours and support for staff who
work from home.

## Hybrid working

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week, with the remaining
days spent in their home office (Melbourne, Sydney, Perth or Auckland). Teams
coordinate their in-office days together so that collaborative work, planning
sessions and onboarding can happen face to face.

## Core hours

Regardless of where an employee is working, everyone is expected to be
available during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 AEST. Meetings that involve
staff across multiple offices should be scheduled inside this window wherever
possible. Outside core hours, employees may arrange their start and finish
times with their manager.

| Arrangement | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Hybrid, up to 3 days remote per week | Manager |
| Fully remote | Executive Leadership Team |

## Fully remote arrangements

Employees who wish to work remotely on a fully remote basis, rather than the
standard hybrid pattern, must obtain approval from the Executive Leadership
Team. Requests are considered on a case-by-case basis and take into account
role requirements, team structure and time zone overlap with core hours.

## Home office allowance

Employees working under a hybrid or fully remote arrangement can claim a
home office allowance of $650 to set up or improve their home workspace. This
allowance can be claimed once every 24 months per employee, and receipts must
be submitted through the People Portal.
