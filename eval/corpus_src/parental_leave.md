# Parental Leave Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes the paid parental leave available to Harbourline
Freight Systems employees on the birth or adoption of a child, and how it
interacts with superannuation contributions during unpaid leave.

## Eligibility

An employee qualifies for paid parental leave once they have completed 12
months of continuous service with Harbourline Freight Systems as at the
expected date of birth or placement. Employees who have not yet reached 12
months of continuous service may still take unpaid parental leave under the
relevant government scheme, but the company-paid entitlements described below
do not apply until that milestone is reached.

## Paid leave entitlement

Employees who are the primary carer of a newborn or newly placed child receive
18 weeks of parental leave at full pay. Employees who are the secondary carer
receive 6 weeks of parental leave at full pay. Both entitlements may be taken
in a single continuous block or, with manager agreement, split into two
separate blocks around the birth or placement.

| Role | Paid leave |
|---|---|
| Primary carer | 18 weeks at full pay |
| Secondary carer | 6 weeks at full pay |

## Superannuation during leave

Superannuation guarantee contributions continue to be paid as normal during
the paid portion of parental leave. In addition, Harbourline continues paying
superannuation contributions on the unpaid portion of parental leave for up to
12 months, so that an employee's retirement savings are not reduced by an
extended period of unpaid leave.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from parental leave are entitled to return to their
previous role, or a comparable role at the same level and pay, and may agree
a phased return with their manager.
