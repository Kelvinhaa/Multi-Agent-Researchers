# On-Call Rotation Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how Harbourline Freight Systems engineering staffs
its on-call rotation, the allowance paid for carrying the pager, and the
limits placed on how often any one engineer is on call.

## Rotation structure

On-call rotations run for 7 days, handing over each Wednesday at 10:00
AEST. Each rotation has a primary responder, who is paged first, and a
secondary responder, who is paged if the primary does not acknowledge an
incident within the response window set out in the Incident Response
Policy. Handover happens at a fixed time regardless of public holidays, so
that the schedule stays predictable across the team.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Primary | First paged for any incident |
| Secondary | Paged if primary does not acknowledge |

## On-call allowance

Engineers rostered on call, whether as primary or secondary, receive a
weekly allowance of $900, paid in the ordinary pay run for the period in
which the rotation falls. The allowance is paid in addition to any incident
overtime that may apply separately under the employee's award or contract.

## Frequency cap

To avoid burnout, no engineer may be rostered for more than 1 rotation in
any rolling 4-week period, whether as primary or secondary. The rotation
schedule is generated automatically from the on-call roster and is checked
against this cap before publication.

## Swaps

Engineers may swap an assigned rotation with a teammate of equivalent
seniority, provided the swap is recorded in the on-call system before the
rotation begins and does not put either engineer over the frequency cap
above.
