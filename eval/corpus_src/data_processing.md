# Data Processing Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how Harbourline Freight Systems processes customer
data on customers' behalf, where that data resides, and how customers are
notified of a data breach or a change to a sub-processor.

## Data residency

Customer data is processed and stored by default in the ap-southeast-2
region. Customers who require their data to remain within the European
Union may elect the eu-west-1 region option instead, which is available at
contract signature or by written request thereafter. Once a customer has
selected a residency option, data is not moved to a different region without
their agreement.

| Residency option | Region |
|---|---|
| Default | ap-southeast-2 |
| EU option | eu-west-1 |

## Breach notification

If Harbourline identifies a data breach affecting customer data, affected
customers are notified within 72 hours of the breach being confirmed. The
notification describes the nature of the breach, the data affected, and the
remediation steps Harbourline is taking, so customers can meet their own
downstream notification obligations.

## Sub-processors

Harbourline maintains a public list of sub-processors used to deliver its
service. Where Harbourline intends to add or replace a sub-processor,
customers are given 30 days notice before the change takes effect, so that
a customer can object or terminate their agreement if the new
sub-processor is unacceptable to them.

## Customer responsibilities

Customers remain responsible for the accuracy of the data they submit to
Harbourline and for ensuring they have the necessary rights to have that
data processed under this policy.
