# Corporate Card Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes who can hold a Harbourline Freight Systems corporate
card, the monthly spending limits attached to each card, and how cardholders
must reconcile their transactions.

## Monthly limits

Standard corporate cards carry a monthly limit of $5,000. Cardholders at
manager level and above are issued a card with a monthly limit of $15,000,
reflecting the larger and more varied spend a manager typically approves on
behalf of their team. Limits are set per cardholder and do not pool across a
team.

| Cardholder level | Monthly limit |
|---|---|
| Standard | $5,000 |
| Manager and above | $15,000 |

## Reconciliation

Every transaction on a corporate card must be reconciled in the Finance
Portal, with a receipt and business purpose attached, within 10 business
days of the transaction date. Reconciliation is the cardholder's
responsibility even where a transaction was made on behalf of someone else.

## Missed reconciliations and suspension

A missed reconciliation is recorded each time a transaction remains
unreconciled past the 10 business day window. After 2 missed
reconciliations, the card is suspended until the cardholder brings all
outstanding transactions up to date with their manager's sign-off. A
suspended card can be reinstated once the backlog is cleared.

| Missed reconciliations | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 2 | Card suspended |

## Card use

Corporate cards are for business expenses only. Personal spend on a
corporate card, even if reimbursed promptly, is treated as a policy breach
and may be escalated to the cardholder's manager and People & Culture.
