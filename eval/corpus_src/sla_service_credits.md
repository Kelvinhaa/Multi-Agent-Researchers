# SLA and Service Credits Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets Harbourline Freight Systems' contractual uptime
commitments to customers, the service credits payable if uptime falls
short, and the contractual response commitment for a P1 incident.

## Uptime commitments

Standard tier customers are contracted to 99.9% monthly uptime. Enterprise
tier customers are contracted to a higher commitment of 99.95% monthly
uptime, reflecting the additional infrastructure redundancy included in
enterprise agreements. Uptime is measured monthly across each customer's
production environment.

| Tier | Contracted uptime |
|---|---|
| Standard | 99.9% |
| Enterprise | 99.95% |

## Service credits

Where actual uptime in a given month falls below the contracted level,
customers are entitled to a service credit against that month's fees. Falling
below 99.9% uptime entitles the customer to a credit of 10% of monthly fees.
Falling below 99.0% entitles the customer to a credit of 25% of monthly
fees. Falling below 95% entitles the customer to a credit of 50% of monthly
fees. Credits are not cumulative across these bands; the customer receives
the single largest credit for which their actual uptime qualifies.

| Uptime achieved | Service credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.9% | 10% of monthly fees |
| Below 99.0% | 25% of monthly fees |
| Below 95% | 50% of monthly fees |

## Contractual incident response

Harbourline's contractual commitment to customers is to respond to a P1
incident within 30 minutes of it being logged. This is the commitment
stated in customer agreements and is separate from Harbourline engineering's
own internal acknowledgement target, which is set out in the Incident
Response Policy.

## Claiming a credit

A customer wishing to claim a service credit must submit the claim within
30 days of the incident giving rise to the shortfall. Claims submitted
after this window are not honoured, so customers are encouraged to raise a
claim as soon as an uptime shortfall is identified rather than waiting for
their monthly statement.
