# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behaviour expected of everyone
at Harbourline Freight Systems, including how to handle gifts, conflicts of
interest and outside work.

## Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept modest gifts and hospitality from customers, suppliers
or partners in the ordinary course of business. Any gift or hospitality
valued above $200 must be declared to the employee's manager and recorded in
the Gifts Register before it is accepted, so that the company can assess
whether it creates a conflict of interest.

| Gift value | Action |
|---|---|
| $200 or below | No declaration required |
| Above $200 | Must be declared before acceptance |

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must declare any actual, potential or perceived conflict of
interest as soon as they become aware of it, and no later than 5 business
days after it arises. Declarations are made through the People Portal and
reviewed by the employee's manager together with People & Culture.

## Secondary employment

Employees who wish to take on secondary employment, consulting work or a
directorship outside Harbourline must obtain written approval before starting
that work. Approval considers whether the outside role competes with
Harbourline, uses company time or resources, or otherwise creates a conflict
of interest.

## Reporting concerns

Employees who become aware of a breach of this Code, including an
undeclared gift or conflict of interest, should raise it with their manager
or through the confidential ethics hotline. Reports are treated
confidentially and without retaliation against the person raising them.
