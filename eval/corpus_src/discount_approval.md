# Discount Approval Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets the approval authority required for a sales discount off
Harbourline Freight Systems' list pricing, so that larger discounts receive
proportionately senior sign-off.

## Approval thresholds

An account executive may approve a discount of up to 10% off list price
without further sign-off. A discount between 10% and 20% requires sales
manager approval. A discount between 20% and 30% requires approval from the
VP of Sales. Any discount above 30% requires joint approval from the CFO
and the CEO before it can be offered to a customer.

| Discount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 10% | Account executive |
| 10%–20% | Sales manager |
| 20%–30% | VP Sales |
| Above 30% | CFO and CEO jointly |

## Requesting approval

Discount requests above an account executive's own authority are logged in
the CRM against the opportunity, with the requested discount and its
business justification, and routed to the appropriate approver
automatically based on the table above.

## Multi-year and bundled deals

Where a deal combines multiple products or spans multiple years, the
discount percentage is assessed against the blended effective discount
across the full deal, not against any single line item, so that a deal
cannot be structured to avoid the correct approval tier.

## Recording approvals

Every approved discount is recorded against the opportunity in the CRM,
including the name of the approver, before the discount can be reflected in
a quote sent to the customer.
